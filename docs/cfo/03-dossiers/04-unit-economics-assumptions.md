## How to Use This Dossier

This dossier documents every material assumption in the unit economics model, with:
- The assumption
- The value or range (where applicable)
- The source or basis for the assumption
- The confidence level
- What would change the assumption

The point is to make the model transparent and challengeable. The CFO's recommendation is only as good as the assumptions behind it — and the most important assumptions are the ones with the lowest confidence and the highest impact.

### F. Strategic assumptions (medium confidence — operator-supplied)

These are strategic assumptions that depend on the operator's judgment and goals. They are the assumptions that shape the strategic economic question (is the time well spent relative to alternatives?).

| # | Assumption | Value / range | Source / basis | Confidence |
|---|-----------|--------------|----------------|------------|
| F1 | Opportunity cost of the operator's time | Personal/strategic valuation. Not a market price. | Operator's judgment | Low (as a number) / high (as a question worth asking) |
| F2 | Zero-budget constraint is (or is not) temporary | Venture decision. If temporary, the path widens; if permanent, the path narrows. | Venture decision | High on the constraint as stated (zero now) / low on whether it's permanent |

**CFO note:** F1 is the most important strategic assumption. The entire economic question — is this channel worth the operator's time? — depends on how the operator values their time and what alternatives they have. The CFO's models can structure the question; the answer is the operator's.

## Assumption Importance Ranking

Ranking the assumptions by (impact × uncertainty) — the ones that matter most and are least certain:

1. **D1 — Audience growth.** Highest impact (precondition for all revenue), lowest confidence. The biggest economic unknown.
2. **B1 — Per-clip time.** High impact (drives cost side), low confidence currently. Most important operational assumption to measure.
3. **C1/C3 — Eligibility on current format.** High impact (gates platform revenue), low-to-medium confidence. The gating question for platform routes.
4. **F1 — Opportunity cost of operator's time.** High strategic impact, low confidence as a number. The strategic economic question.
5. **C5 — Sensitive-content flags on war clips.** High impact on war-clip viability, medium confidence on the risk, medium-to-low on which clips. The fastest failure mode.
6. **E4 — Sponsorship revenue potential.** Medium-to-high long-term impact, low confidence (depends on audience and fit).
7. **A2/A3 — Genre and format.** High confidence as facts, but the implications (advertiser-friendliness, sponsor fit, sensitivity) are medium confidence.
