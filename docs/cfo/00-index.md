## Confidence Convention

- **High confidence:** Well-established rule, widely documented, low controversy (e.g., YPP has eligibility thresholds; reused-content rules exist).
- **Medium confidence:** Established framework with fact-specific application or evolving detail (e.g., exact current thresholds, how a specific content pattern will be treated).
- **Low confidence:** Uncertain, evolving, dependent on facts not yet known, or dependent on future platform behavior.

## Explicit Assumptions Carried Into This Engagement

These are stated up front so they can be challenged. They are documented with confidence levels in L3.

1. **Zero budget is real.** No licensing money, no tooling spend, no outsourcing spend to start. The models must reflect this.
2. **Monetization is prioritized ASAP** — but ASAP does not mean day one. The models must distinguish "plan for it now" from "expect it now."
3. **Genre is war/news + tech + viral, highly clickable.** This affects sponsor surface, sensitivity profile, and platform treatment. The models should not assume war-only or entertainment-only economics.
4. **Format is cut + text overlay, no voiceover, no on-camera (for now).** This affects transformation posture, platform perception, and monetization eligibility.
5. **Accounts are live on TikTok, YouTube, Instagram.** The channel is not starting from zero accounts, but it is starting from zero audience and zero monetization history.
6. **US-centric copyright and platform context, with international as a secondary uncertainty.** The models center US copyright and US-platform context; international exposure is flagged where it matters.
