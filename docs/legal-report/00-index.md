## What This Is

A legal and risk landscape assessment for a zero-budget trending clips channel that:
- Clips existing third-party war/news footage (broadcast, agency wire, other creators)
- Publishes cut + text overlay clips (headlines, captions, context lines, timestamps)
- Targets TikTok → YouTube Shorts → Instagram Reels, accounts already live
- Has monetization prioritized ASAP
- Has a small empirical test batch first

This report is NOT legal advice. It does NOT conclude "this is safe" or "this is legal." It maps the landscape, identifies the material risks, and flags where confidence is high, medium, or low.

## Pyramid Structure

- `01-summary/` — L1: the headline landscape, the binding constraints, the risk posture in plain language
- `02-analysis/` — L2: per-domain analysis — copyright/fair use, platform ToS, news footage licensing, graphic/sensitive content, attribution myth, sanctions/geo, risk-mitigation patterns, zero-budget constraints

## How to Read

Start at `01-summary/landscape.md`. Then read the L2 analysis files that match your question. L3 dossiers are reference material pulled on demand.

## Confidence Convention

- **High confidence:** Well-established principle, widely agreed, low controversy (e.g., "attribution alone does not immunize you")
- **Medium confidence:** Established framework with fact-specific application (e.g., fair use four-factor analysis — the framework is established, the outcome per clip is not)
- **Low confidence:** Uncertain, evolving, jurisdiction-dependent, or dependent on facts not yet known (e.g., specific platform enforcement behavior for a particular content type)

## Key Questions This Report Answers

1. What are the material legal and platform risks for this specific model?
2. Which risks are binding constraints vs. background noise?
3. What does "text overlay" actually buy in terms of transformation, and what does it not buy?
4. What does zero budget force on the legal posture?
5. Where is the line between a defensible fair-use argument and "just infringement"?
6. What should the build plan do to reduce exposure without licensing money?
