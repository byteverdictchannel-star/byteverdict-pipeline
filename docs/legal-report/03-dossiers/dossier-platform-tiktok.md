## TikTok Terms of Service — Key Excerpts (verbatim)

*Source: TikTok Terms of Service, https://www.tiktok.com/legal/terms-of-service, last updated July 15, 2026. Retrieved 2026-08-24.*

### 1. Scope and additional policies

> "These Terms govern your use of our services, which include TikTok applications, websites, software (e.g. the TikTok embeddable media player), features, technologies, and related services... accessed or delivered via any platform or device (the 'Platform')."

> "Your use of the Platform is also governed by our Privacy Policy and Community Guidelines. And, depending on the specific services you use, the additional terms and policies listed below or that are otherwise made available to you may apply ('Additional Terms')."

Relevant Additional Terms referenced:
- Intellectual Property Policy — "This policy applies to all content on the Platform that is protected by intellectual property rights."
- Rewards Policy — "This policy governs the virtual and physical rewards you may be eligible to receive when you participate in an incentive program, promotion, and/or campaign offered by TikTok USDS Joint Venture."
- Branded Content Policy — "This policy applies when you post content on the Platform that promotes a third-party brand or its products or services in exchange for payment or any other incentive."
- Commercial Music Library Terms — for commercial entities posting music from the Commercial Music Library.

### 2. Enforcement

> "We want the platform to be a safe place, where people can be who they are and have fun. To help protect you and others in our community, we work with our affiliates, service providers, and business partners who may use a mix of technology (including through automated means), human moderation, and reports from our users and others to identify suspected violations of these Terms, our Community Guidelines, and other conditions or policies."

### 3. Account and username

> "We may revoke, reclaim, and/or reassign the username of your account in certain circumstances, such as, when you have not logged into your account for 180 days, if we ban your account, or if we reasonably believe that your username violates our Terms, Community Guidelines, or other conditions or policies, and/or interferes with or infringes upon the rights of other users."

### 4. Dispute resolution

> "These Terms and any claims, causes of action, of any kind or character, or demand arising out of or relating to the Terms will be governed by the laws of the State of California... Any claim, cause of action or dispute, arising out of or relating to these Terms or the Platform shall also be resolved exclusively in the U.S. District Court for the Central District of California or the Superior Court of the State of California, County of Los Angeles..."

> "YOU AND TIKTOK USDS JOINT VENTURE AGREE THAT YOU MUST INITIATE ANY PROCEEDING OR ACTION WITHIN ONE (1) YEAR OF THE DATE OF THE OCCURRENCE OF THE EVENT OR FACTS GIVING RISE TO A DISPUTE..."

## TikTok Community Guidelines — Structural Landscape (not verbatim)

*Page blocked for automated extraction at capture time. The following is the structural landscape as understood from the platform's publicly stated frameworks, used in the analysis. It is a summary, not a verbatim source.*

TikTok's Community Guidelines address content categories including, at relevant times and in relevant versions:
- Violent and graphic content (including depictions of death, injury, gore, and disturbing content)
- Dangerous acts and challenges
- Hate speech and harassment
- Harmful or misleading content

TikTok also operates creator reward / monetization programs with their own eligibility criteria (referenced via the Rewards Policy and related program terms), which commonly include requirements around account standing, originality/reused-content expectations, and compliance with platform policies.

**Implication for the channel:** The exact current text of the Community Guidelines and the specific monetization program terms should be re-read at the time of any operational decision, because platform policies evolve. The structural point — that TikTok enforces violence/graphic content and reused-content/monetization-eligibility rules independently of copyright — is stable even if the precise thresholds shift.

## Why These Excerpts Matter for the Channel

1. **The ToS makes Community Guidelines and the Intellectual Property Policy additional, binding terms.** Using TikTok means agreeing to those too — they are not separate from the platform relationship.
2. **Enforcement is described as a mix of automated technology, human moderation, and user reports.** This is the structural basis for the analysis's point that platform enforcement is partly automated and partly opaque.
3. **Monetization/rewards programs have their own policies.** Eligibility is not automatic and is governed by separate program terms that can include originality and compliance requirements.
4. **California law and exclusive venue.** Any contractual dispute with TikTok would likely be governed by California law in specified California courts — relevant if the channel ever faces a ToS-level dispute (as distinct from a copyright dispute).
