## Meta Terms of Service — Key Excerpts (verbatim)

*Source: Meta Terms of Service, https://www.facebook.com/terms, effective January 1, 2025. Retrieved 2026-08-24.*

### 1. Scope and separate terms

> "These Terms of Service (the 'Terms') govern your access and use of Facebook, Messenger, and the other products, websites, features, apps, services, technologies, and software we offer (the 'Meta Products' or Products), except where we expressly state that separate terms (and not these) apply. (For example, your use of Instagram is subject to the Instagram Terms of Use)."

### 2. Safety, security, and integrity

> "People will only build community on Meta Products if they feel safe and secure. We work hard to maintain the security (including the availability, authenticity, integrity, and confidentiality) of our Products and services. We employ dedicated teams around the world, work with external service providers, partners and other relevant entities and develop advanced technical systems to detect potential misuse of our Products, harmful conduct towards others, and situations where we may be able to help support or protect our community, including to respond to user reports of potentially violating content."

> "If we learn of content or conduct like this, we may take appropriate action based on our assessment that may include - notifying you, offering help, removing content, removing or restricting access to certain features, disabling an account, or contacting law enforcement."

### 3. Content removal and restriction language

> "We may take appropriate action based on our assessment that may include - notifying you, offering help, removing content, removing or restricting access to certain features, disabling an account, or contacting law enforcement."

(This is the structural basis for the analysis's point that platforms can remove content, restrict access to features, or disable accounts for policy violations.)

### 4. Other terms and policies

The Meta Terms list many additional policies that may apply:
- Community Standards — "These guidelines outline our standards regarding the content you post to Facebook and your activity on Facebook and other Meta Products."
- Commercial Terms — for commercial/business use.
- Community Payment Terms — for payments on Meta Products.
- Commerce Policies — for selling goods/services on Facebook, Instagram, WhatsApp.
- Music Guidelines — for content containing music.
- Advertising Policies — for advertisers.
- Live Policies — for Facebook Live.
- Recommendations Guidelines — for content recommendation.

### 5. Global infrastructure

> "To operate our global services and enable you to connect with people around the world, we need to transfer, store and distribute content and data to our data centers, partners, service providers, vendors and systems around the world, including outside your country of residence."

## Instagram-Specific Framework — Structural Landscape

*The Instagram Help Center page (https://help.instagram.com/477434105313145) was not available at capture time ("This Page Isn't Available"). The following is the structural landscape, not a verbatim capture.*

Instagram's relevant frameworks for the channel include:
- **Instagram Terms of Use** — separate from the Meta/Facebook ToS; governs Instagram use.
- **Community Guidelines / Community Standards** — content standards, including for violent/graphic content and other categories.
- **Reels-specific distribution and features** — Reels feed, remix, and other Reels features.
- **Monetization features** — various Instagram monetization paths (bonuses, gifts, subscriptions, branded content, and other program-specific features), each with its own eligibility and policy requirements.
- **Copyright/IP frameworks** — Instagram's IP/content policies.

**Implication for the channel:** Instagram is described in the brief as a distribution surface with a different audience, not the primary monetization anchor. The relevant risk is more about distribution health, account standing, and reach than about a primary monetization gate — but Instagram's reused-content and sensitive-content rules still matter, and monetization features have their own eligibility.

## Why These Excerpts Matter for the Channel

1. **Instagram is governed by separate Instagram Terms of Use.** Using Instagram means agreeing to those terms, not just the Meta/Facebook ToS. The separate terms should be read directly for operational decisions.

2. **The Meta ToS explicitly authorizes content removal, feature restriction, and account disabling for policy violations.** This is the contractual basis for the analysis's point that platforms can remove content and restrict accounts for policy reasons.

3. **Safety/integrity enforcement uses "advanced technical systems" and teams.** The structural basis for the analysis's point that platform enforcement is partly automated and partly human.

4. **Community Standards apply to "content you post" and "your activity."** Reused-content and activity-pattern issues can fall under this umbrella.

5. **Payments and commerce are governed by separate terms.** If the channel develops direct monetization (tips, paid content, commerce), the Community Payment Terms and Commerce Policies become relevant in addition to the content terms.
