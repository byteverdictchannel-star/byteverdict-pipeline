## What This Is

A meticulous zero-budget build plan for a trending clips channel that:
- Clips existing third-party war/news footage (broadcast, agency wire, other creators)
- Publishes cut + text overlay clips (headlines, captions, context lines, timestamps)
- Targets TikTok → YouTube Shorts → Instagram Reels, accounts already live
- Launches with a small empirical test batch (a handful over 1–2 weeks)
- Has monetization prioritized ASAP

This plan is grounded in the locked-in decisions in the planning brief and in the legal/risk landscape report (see `~/clips-channel/legal-report/00-index.md`). It does not assume licensing money. It assumes free tooling and a solo operator, with subagent help where it fits.

## Explicit Invariants

- Zero budget: no licensing money. The plan is honest about where that forces reliance on fair-use arguments vs. where it just limits scale.
- Test batch is small and empirical. Do not design for a cadence the channel hasn't earned yet.
- War/news content gets elevated platform scrutiny independent of copyright. Platform-side risk may be the binding constraint.
- Monetization is prioritized ASAP — plan reflects platform thresholds, off-platform ownership, and sponsor-readiness structure.
