ByteVerdict — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-08-26

ByteVerdict is a news and commentary media brand. The ByteVerdict publishing application ("the App") is a server-side automation tool that posts video content to the ByteVerdict Facebook Page, which is owned and operated by the same individual who controls the App. The App does not offer a public login, does not collect personal data from Facebook users, and does not process data on behalf of third parties.

Data we access

  • The App uses Facebook Page permissions solely to publish content to the ByteVerdict Page that the App owner administrates.
  • No end-user Facebook profile data is read, stored, or shared.

Data retention

The App stores only its own access tokens (required to publish) in a private, access-controlled environment. No user content is retained beyond what is publicly published to the ByteVerdict Page.

Contact

For any privacy question or to request removal of data, contact byteverdictchannel@gmail.com.
