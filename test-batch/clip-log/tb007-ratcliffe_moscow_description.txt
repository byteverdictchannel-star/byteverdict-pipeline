America's top spy just landed in Moscow. No one would say why.

A US military plane left Washington without the usual notices and touched down with no manifest, no names, and no explanation. Neither the US nor Russia would confirm who was actually on board — even after reporters asked the Kremlin spokesman directly.

DW News reports this was CIA Director John Ratcliffe making a secret visit to Russia. The last sitting CIA chief to go to Moscow was in 2021, right before Putin rolled tanks into Ukraine. The timing alone raises questions.
