Trump's election-interference case just got a new indictment. A revised one.

Special counsel Jack Smith filed it August 27 — same 4 core charges against Trump for trying to overturn the 2020 election. But this version strips out some acts the Supreme Court said were off-limits and immune from prosecution.

Trump already called it election interference, posted on Truth Social that Jack Smith "should be ashamed of himself," and his team plans to fight this one too. The debate and the election are both right around the corner — and neither indictment so far has kept him off the campaign trail.

NBC News' Laura Jarrett reports the case was marred in appeals and delays after the Supreme Court's July ruling that Trump cannot be prosecuted for official acts as president. The lower court now has to parse out what he can be prosecuted for — if anything.
